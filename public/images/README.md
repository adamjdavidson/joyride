# Image provenance

`buddha.png` is an AI-generated jade figurine illustration used by the earlier
conversation pilot.

## Mode objects

`firmware/mode-display/assets/{download,expand,challenge,structure,implement}.png`
are the five project-supplied 256px object artworks. Their original creator/tool
is not recorded in the source handoff; they are provided here at the project
owner’s request. They are illustrations rather than photographs of finished
objects. The manifest records source hashes and the derived screen palette.

## Historical perspective frames

`firmware/perspective-display/assets/` contains generated illustrations of Jane
Jacobs, James Baldwin, Lucille Ball, Niccolò Machiavelli and Vasubandhu, plus their
monochrome display frames. The original project used image generation with visual
references; no source photographs, mixed-person contact sheets or private image
metadata are included. The Vasubandhu likeness follows a later sculptural
tradition, not an authenticated lifetime portrait. The `-176.png` suffix is a
legacy filename; use actual image dimensions rather than inferring them from it.

The [perspective guide](../../docs/PERSPECTIVES.md) links two external public-domain
images: a Freud photograph by Max Halberstadt (circa 1921) and a Leonardo portrait
attributed to Francesco Melzi (circa 1515–1518). Exact source and rights pages are
credited beside each image. They are documentation illustrations, not bundled
firmware frames.

Manufacturer/supplier photographs in [the objects guide](../../docs/OBJECTS.md)
are externally hosted, credited references and retain their respective rights.
The software MIT license does not replace third-party image rights or establish
exclusive rights in generated artwork.
